# Code of Conduct

OpenCareFlow follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming and technically constructive community. Contributors should assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid sharing sensitive patient, employee or hospital information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate these expectations.

Until a dedicated project email exists, conduct incidents should be reported privately to the repository owner rather than opened as public issues.
